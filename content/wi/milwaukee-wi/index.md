## 1. Immediate Steps After a Crash in Milwaukee, Wisconsin

### Stay Calm and Ensure Safety

The moments following a car accident can be chaotic, but it's crucial to remain as calm as possible. First, check yourself and passengers for injuries. If anyone is hurt, call 911 immediately to request medical assistance. Your safety and health are the top priority.

If the accident occurs on a busy road like I-94 or the Milwaukee River Parkway, move to a safe location if possible. This could mean pulling over to the shoulder or moving to a nearby parking lot. Turn on your hazard lights to alert other drivers of your presence.

- Check for injuries and call 911 if necessary.

- Move to a safe location if possible.

- Turn on hazard lights.


### Exchange Information

After ensuring everyone's safety, exchange information with the other driver(s) involved. This includes names, contact details, insurance information, and vehicle registration numbers. In Wisconsin, it's also advisable to note the make, model, and color of the vehicles involved.

If there are any witnesses to the accident, politely ask for their contact information as well. Witnesses can provide valuable third-party accounts of the incident, which may be useful later on.

- Exchange names, contact information, and insurance details.

- Note vehicle information: make, model, color.

- Collect witness contact information if available.


### Document the Scene

Take photos of the accident scene, including all vehicles involved, any visible damages, and the surrounding area. These photographs can serve as crucial evidence if there are disputes about what happened.

It's also helpful to jot down notes about the accident while the details are still fresh in your mind. Include the time and date, weather conditions, and any other pertinent information.

- Photograph the scene and damages.

- Take notes on the accident details.

- Record weather and road conditions.


### Report the Accident

In Milwaukee, you are required to report any accident that results in injury, death, or significant property damage to the police. Contact the Milwaukee Police Department at (414) 933-4444 to file a report. This can also be done online through their official website.

Additionally, you may need to report the accident to the Wisconsin Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) if it meets certain criteria, such as exceeding a specific amount in damages. For more detailed requirements, consult the Wisconsin DMV website or contact them directly at (608) 264-7447.

- Contact Milwaukee Police Department: (414) 933-4444.

- File a report if necessary with the Wisconsin DMV.

- Check online resources for reporting requirements.


## 2. Milwaukee-Specific Information You Must Know

### Understanding Wisconsin Accident Laws

Wisconsin operates under an at-fault system for car accidents, meaning the driver responsible for the accident is liable for damages. Understanding how fault is determined is crucial, as it affects insurance claims and potential legal actions.

Fault can be influenced by various factors such as traffic violations, witness statements, and police reports. It's essential to gather as much evidence as possible to support your position, whether you're filing a claim or defending against one.

- Wisconsin is an at-fault state.

- Fault affects insurance and legal claims.

- Gather evidence to support your case.


### Navigating Milwaukee's Highways

Milwaukee's highways, including the I-94 and I-43 interchange, are some of the busiest in the state. Accidents in these areas can be particularly hazardous due to high traffic volumes and complex road layouts.

Familiarize yourself with local hotspots like North Avenue and Oakland Avenue and Capitol Drive and Fond du Lac Avenue. These intersections are known for frequent accidents and require extra caution.

- I-94 and I-43 are major traffic arteries.

- High traffic volumes increase accident risks.

- Be cautious at known accident hotspots.


### Seeking Medical Attention in Milwaukee

After a car accident, it's important to seek medical attention even if you feel fine. Some injuries, like whiplash or internal bleeding, may not be immediately apparent. Milwaukee offers several trauma centers, such as Froedtert Hospital and Aurora St. Luke's Medical Center, that provide comprehensive emergency care.

For children involved in accidents, Children's Wisconsin is a specialized facility offering pediatric care. Prompt medical evaluation can not only safeguard your health but also document injuries for insurance and legal purposes.

- Seek medical attention promptly.

- Visit Froedtert Hospital or Aurora St. Luke's for emergency care.

- Children's Wisconsin specializes in pediatric trauma.


### Wisconsin Insurance Requirements

Wisconsin requires drivers to carry liability insurance with minimum coverage limits of $25,000 for injury or death per person, $50,000 per accident, and $10,000 for property damage. Additionally, uninsured motorist coverage is mandatory with minimum limits of $25,000 per person and $50,000 per accident.

Unlike some states, Wisconsin does not require Personal Injury Protection (PIP) insurance, as it follows an at-fault system. Understanding these requirements is essential for ensuring compliance and protecting yourself financially.

- Liability insurance: $25,000/$50,000/$10,000 minimums.

- Uninsured motorist coverage is mandatory.

- No PIP requirement as Wisconsin is an at-fault state.


## 3. How to Deal with Insurance Companies After a Milwaukee Crash

### Communicating with Insurers

After an accident, you will need to contact your insurance company to report the incident. Provide them with accurate details about the accident, but avoid admitting fault or speculating about the cause. Stick to the facts and let the evidence speak for itself.

It's common for insurance adjusters to contact you for more information. Be prepared to answer their questions, but remember you are not obligated to provide a recorded statement without consulting a legal professional.

- Report the accident promptly to your insurer.

- Provide factual information only.

- Consult a lawyer before giving recorded statements.


### Negotiating Settlements

Insurance companies may offer a settlement to quickly resolve your claim. While it might be tempting to accept the first offer, it's important to evaluate whether it fully covers your damages and injuries. Consulting with a licensed attorney in Wisconsin can help you understand the fair value of your claim.

Consider factors such as medical expenses, property damage, lost wages, and pain and suffering when negotiating a settlement. Having a comprehensive understanding of your losses will empower you in discussions with insurance adjusters.

- Evaluate settlement offers carefully.

- Consider all aspects of your damages.

- Seek legal advice to understand claim value.


### Dealing with Denied Claims

If your insurance claim is denied, don't panic. Insurance companies are required to provide a reason for denial, which you can contest if you believe it is unjustified. Familiarize yourself with your policy details and gather evidence to support your case.

Consulting with a legal expert can provide guidance on how to proceed, whether through negotiation or pursuing legal action. Understanding your rights and options is crucial in these situations.

- Understand reasons for claim denial.

- Review your policy and gather supporting evidence.

- Consult a legal expert for guidance.


## 4. Common Injuries & What to Expect

### Identifying Common Injuries

Car accidents can result in a wide range of injuries, from minor bruises to serious conditions. Common injuries include whiplash, concussions, broken bones, and soft tissue injuries. It's important to recognize symptoms early and seek medical evaluation to prevent further complications.

Even if injuries seem minor, documenting them is essential for insurance claims and potential legal actions. Medical records serve as evidence of the injuries sustained and their impact on your life.

- Whiplash and concussions are common.

- Look for symptoms like pain, dizziness, or numbness.

- Document all injuries with medical records.


### Long-Term Effects and Rehabilitation

Some injuries from car accidents may have long-term effects that require ongoing treatment or rehabilitation. This can include physical therapy, surgery, or counseling for emotional trauma. Understanding the potential long-term impacts is crucial for managing your recovery and financial planning.

Discussing your prognosis with healthcare providers will help you understand the timeline and extent of your recovery. This information is also valuable when negotiating settlements to ensure future medical needs are covered.

- Consider potential long-term impacts of injuries.

- Plan for ongoing treatment and rehabilitation.

- Use medical prognosis in settlement negotiations.
